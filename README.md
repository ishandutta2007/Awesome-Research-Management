# Awesome-Research-Management

## Top Research Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Research Information Management (RIM/CRIS), Researcher Profiles, Publications, Outputs, Repositories & Institutional Reporting*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Research Management** (also known as Research Information Management or Current Research Information Systems). These systems help institutions manage researcher profiles, publications, datasets, grants, activities, and reporting while supporting open science and repository functions.



**Examples** include Symplectic Elements, Elsevier Pure, Ex Libris Esploro, InfoReady, Pivot-RP, Digital Commons, Converis, Interfolio Faculty180, Dimensions, and Figshare (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Strong open options exist. **VIVO**, **DSpace-CRIS**, **InvenioRDM**, and related projects provide production-ready research information and repository capabilities used by universities worldwide. This section is heavily expanded with these tools.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[VIVO](https://github.com/vivo-project)**  

  Leading open-source research information management system for representing scholarship and making researcher profiles and outputs discoverable on the semantic web.



- **[DSpace-CRIS](https://wiki.lyrasis.org/display/DSPACECRIS)**  

  Open-source extension of DSpace that adds full CRIS/RIM functionality while keeping the institutional repository at its core.



- **[InvenioRDM](https://inveniosoftware.org/products/rdm/)**  

  Turn-key open-source research data management repository platform based on the Invenio framework and Zenodo experience.



- **[DSpace](https://github.com/DSpace/DSpace)**  

  Widely deployed open-source institutional repository software often used as the foundation for research output management.



- **[Profiles Research Networking Software](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source research networking and profiling system used by a number of academic medical centers and institutions.



- **[Samvera / Hyrax and related repository frameworks](https://github.com/samvera)**  

  Open-source digital repository frameworks used for research data and institutional collections.



- **[OpenAIRE and CERIF-related open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects supporting European research information standards and interoperability.



- **[ORCID integration and profile synchronization open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools that help institutions keep researcher profiles synchronized with ORCID and other persistent identifier systems.



- **[Research graph and discovery open projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Community efforts to link publications, datasets, grants, and people in open research graphs.



- **[Custom CRIS implementations on open repository stacks](https://github.com/)**  

  Institutional adaptations that combine DSpace, Invenio, or VIVO with local reporting and faculty activity needs.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **VIVO** for semantic researcher profiles and research discovery.

- Choosing **DSpace-CRIS** when you want a combined repository + CRIS in one open platform.

- Using **InvenioRDM** for modern research data management with strong FAIR support.

- Combining open repositories with external analytics (OpenAlex, Dimensions APIs where available) for reporting.

- Accepting that polished commercial analytics, deep publisher integrations, and turnkey multi-module enterprise suites still favor Pure, Elements, Esploro, and Converis for many research-intensive institutions.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Stand up VIVO or DSpace-CRIS for profiles and outputs → deposit datasets and publications in InvenioRDM or DSpace → synchronize with ORCID → generate institutional reports from the open data model. This stack is fully open and production-proven at many universities. Commercial platforms (Symplectic Elements, Elsevier Pure, Ex Libris Esploro, Converis, Figshare, Dimensions, etc.) remain the practical choice when institutions need extensive support, advanced analytics, or tight integration with publisher and funding data ecosystems.
